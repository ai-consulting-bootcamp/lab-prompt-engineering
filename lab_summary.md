In this lab, I tested three customer-service tasks—sentiment analysis, product description generation, and data extraction—starting with simple zero-shot prompts and using repeated runs to identify inconsistencies. The initial prompts produced varying output formats, product descriptions frequently invented nutritional values and other product details, and data extraction sometimes used incorrect field labels. I improved the prompts with clearer instructions, output constraints, few-shot examples, structured JSON, and step-by-step extraction guidance. In the final 15-run tests, the sentiment prompt returned the same single-word classification every time, product descriptions became much shorter and more structured without invented numerical nutritional values, and data extraction consistently produced valid JSON. However, unsupported product claims and occasional extraction errors remained, especially when testing different inputs. Next time, I would test a wider range of cases, validate structured outputs more thoroughly, and evaluate individual prompt changes separately to identify which techniques contribute most to reliability.
